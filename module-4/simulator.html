What this teaches

  • Failures combine. Each factor alone is survivable; together they aren't. Sweep, don't spot-check.
  • An envelope is code, outside the agent: state limits, rate limits and stop-and-alert conditions.
  • Every limit has a cost. Count the fine actions it blocks as well as the unsafe ones it stops.
  • An envelope inherits its model's blind spots. Checking that the twin still matches the building is part of the envelope.

This is a simplified browser model for building intuition, not the real build: the floor is a simple thermal formula, and the "agent" is a scripted list of proposals, not a language model. Your HOS 4 build in Codespaces is the real thing. Back to the Module 4 lesson · HOS 4 guide.
