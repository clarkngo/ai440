What this teaches

  • Error at the sensors says little. Compare models between the sensors and past the data, where the twin is used.
  • Physics rules out the impossible: nothing in the wall can be colder than the outside surface.
  • λ is a balance. At 0 you have a data-only network; very high, the physics wins even when it's wrong.
  • Wrong physics is confidently wrong. Learning the uncertain parameter from the data is often the fix.

This is a simplified browser model for building intuition, not the real build: the "PINN" is a physics-shaped fit rather than a trained network, and the wall is idealized (one material, no moisture). Your AIC 2 build in Codespaces is the real thing. Back to the Module 2 lesson · AIC 2 build guide.
